# Gains & Losses Visualization

This module generates an interactive visualization of thermal gains and losses
by space and parameter (solar gains, internal gains, envelope losses, infiltration, etc.).

The visualization uses **Bokeh** with multiple subplots per zone or per gain type.

---

## 📥 Input Format

Automatically generated by the Manager:

GainsAndLosses.xlsx


Expected structure:

| Space | Solar Gains | Internal Gains | Envelope Losses | ... |
|-------|-------------|----------------|-----------------|-----|

- All values are **monthly aggregates**
- Units typically in **kWh/m²**

---

## 📊 Output

The module produces:

GainsAndLosses.html


Features:
- stacked bars or grouped bars
- hover tools
- zone selector (optional)
- interactive zoom & pan

---

## 🧩 Dependencies

- Bokeh
- Pandas
- Numpy
- Colorcet

---
