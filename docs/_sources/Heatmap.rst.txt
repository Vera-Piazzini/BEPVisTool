Heatmap Visualization
======================

Overview
--------

This module generates **hourly or monthly heatmaps** for any environmental 
or energy variable processed by the BEPVis Manager.  
Typical applications include:

- indoor air temperature
- relative humidity
- energy consumption
- thermal gains/losses
- environmental monitoring data

The heatmap provides an intuitive overview of **temporal patterns**, 
helping users quickly identify anomalies, seasonal behavior, 
system inefficiencies, or comfort deviations.
