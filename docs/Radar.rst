Radar chart - Primary Energy Breakdown
=======================================

Overview
--------

The Radar Chart visualizes the **primary energy consumption profile** of each building space.  
It highlights the relative contribution of different energy categories (heating, cooling, lighting, equipment, etc.) and allows comparing the energy signature of spaces through a combined **polar radar plot + bar chart** layout.

This representation helps identify dominant energy uses, imbalances between spaces, and potential targets for optimization.
